S3 storage classes:
1. standard general purpose:
- for daily, frequently accessed data
- low latency
- big data analysis, mobile apps
- most expensive

2. infrequent access : for less accesed data
a. standard : 3 minimum replications of data, for backups
b. one zone : only 1 replication of data, for secondary backup/ recreatable data

3. glacier : for rarely accessed data
a. instant retrieval : immediate retrieval
b. flexible retrival : within short time frame
c. deep archive : longer time frame, cheapest

3. intelligent tiering:
- monitor access pattern and auto allocates tier
- monthly fee for monitoring
